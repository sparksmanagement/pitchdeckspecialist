## The problem we solve (for the Problem slide)

1. **Cannabis can't advertise like everyone else.** Google Ads, Meta, and most ad
   networks restrict cannabis. The customer journey lives on Weedmaps, Leafly, Yelp,
   and Google Business Profile — pay-to-play marketplaces with auction-based bidding.
2. **Listings decay.** Menus drift out of sync with POS, deals expire, photos go stale,
   hours are wrong, reviews go unanswered, products sit unverified. Every gap costs
   clicks and orders.
3. **Spend is unmanaged.** Platform reps are paid to grow platform revenue, not the
   dispensary's ROAS. Budgets get set once and never optimized.
4. **Nobody owns it.** Budtenders and GMs are running four dashboards on the side.
   No attribution, no dashboard, no one accountable for the number.

## The solution (Solution / Product slides)

Sparks takes full ownership of listing management on the retailer's side and aligns
marketplace activity with business outcomes (orders, revenue, ROAS) instead of
platform-driven spend.

### Seven service pillars (applied on every platform we manage)

| Pillar | What we do |
|---|---|
| Spend Management | Bid, budget, and negotiate paid placements on every platform; optimize for ROAS, not impressions |
| Listing Management | Hours, address, description, photos, attributes, Q&A kept accurate and conversion-optimized |
| Menu Management | Products verified, pricing/availability synced to POS, images and descriptions updated, best-sellers featured |
| Review Management | Same-day responses; removal requests for guideline-breaking reviews; cross-platform review dashboard |
| Deal Management | Daily and monthly promo activations, BOGOs, events, Google Posts, seasonal campaigns |
| Graphic Management | In-house creative for every ad format and listing asset |
| Data Management | One dashboard tracking on- and off-platform conversions; POS integrations (Treez, Dutchie and others) for true revenue attribution |

## Case studies

### Purple Lotus — San Jose, CA (dispensary)
- Situation: 10+ years on Weedmaps, Leafly, Yelp; hundreds of thousands spent with
  weak results.
- What Sparks did: reorganized ad positions, adjusted pricing and ranks, rebuilt deal
  strategy, bought display ads, aligned mission/deals/differentiators across platforms,
  verified every product.
- Results: conversion rate +63%, cost per listing-page visitor down, on-platform ROAS
  doubled within 3 months, return doubled within 6 months.

### Nuna Harvest — New York (dispensary + delivery)
- Results after 6 months: Weedmaps ROAS +638%, Leafly ROAS +44%, Yelp leads +109%.
